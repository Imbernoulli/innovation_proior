A population-based optimizer that looks strong "on average" is usually being carried by the landscapes it happens to fit, and the aggregate used here makes that free ride impossible: the four settings are combined multiplicatively (a geometric mean over rastrigin-30d, rosenbrock-30d, ackley-30d and rastrigin-100d), so one collapsed setting drags the whole score toward zero. The object of study in this variant is therefore the floor, not the mean.

Your objective is worst-landscape robustness under a single fixed, restart-free configuration. Build one evolutionary loop — one operator set, one hyperparameter setting — whose weakest benchmark is as strong as possible. The settings pull in deliberately incompatible directions: separable multimodality at two very different dimensionalities, a narrow curved ill-conditioned valley, and an exponentially flat basin ringed with shallow traps. Exploration aggressive enough to escape local minima tends to stall in the valley; careful exploitation does the reverse; and behaviour tuned at 30 dimensions routinely degrades at 100, so dimensionality has to be an input the loop responds to rather than a constant it was tuned at.

Three constraints define the variant. First, one configuration everywhere: no per-function or per-dimension branching, no fingerprinting of the objective; any adaptation must be driven by statistics the run measures about itself — success rates, improvement velocity, population spread — applied as continuous quantities. Second, no restarts: each run is a single population trajectory, so premature convergence must be prevented by the operator design, not undone by re-initialisation. Third, the evaluation budget implied by the harness (the initial population plus at most pop_size offspring evaluations per generation for n_generations) is a hard cap that any restructured loop must account for explicitly.

What must survive scrutiny at the end is a mechanism: what stopped the weakest landscape from collapsing, evidenced by the run's own adaptation trace, and why it did not cost the other three settings.
